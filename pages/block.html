Roadmap: Blockchain Developer

Title: Roadmap to Becoming a Blockchain Developer

Details: Learn the fundamentals of blockchain technology, smart contracts, and decentralized applications (DApps). Familiarize yourself with various blockchain platforms such as Ethereum, Solana, and Hyperledger.

Key Skills to Master:

  • Blockchain Basics and Distributed Ledger Technology
  • Smart Contracts and Solidity
  • Cryptocurrency and Consensus Algorithms
  • Decentralized Applications (DApps)
  • Blockchain Platforms (Ethereum, Solana, Hyperledger)
  • Security and Blockchain Best Practices
  • Peer-to-Peer Networking

Companies Hiring for Blockchain Developers:

  • Coinbase
  • Binance
  • Ethereum Foundation
  • IBM
  • Accenture

Future Scope:

  • DeFi (Decentralized Finance)
  • NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens)
  • Smart Cities
  • Supply Chain Management
  • Blockchain for Voting and Identity Verification
