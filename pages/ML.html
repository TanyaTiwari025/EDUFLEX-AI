Roadmap: AI/ML Engineer

Title: Roadmap to Becoming an AI/ML Engineer

Details: Specialize in creating algorithms, models, and systems that enable machines to perform tasks without explicit programming. Knowledge of statistics, data analysis, and AI frameworks is key.

Key Skills to Master:

  • Machine Learning Algorithms (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement Learning)
  • Deep Learning and Neural Networks
  • Data Preprocessing and Feature Engineering
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • AI Frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn)
  • Mathematics (Linear Algebra, Probability, Statistics)
  • Big Data Technologies (Spark, Hadoop)

Companies Hiring for AI/ML Engineers:

  • Google
  • Amazon
  • Microsoft
  • Facebook
  • IBM

Future Scope:

  • Autonomous Systems
  • AI for Healthcare
  • AI in Robotics
  • AI for Cybersecurity
  • AI Ethics and Fairness
