Roadmap: UI/UX Designer

Title: Roadmap to Becoming a UI/UX Designer

Details: Specialize in user-centered design principles, usability testing, wireframing, and prototyping to create intuitive and aesthetically pleasing user interfaces.

Key Skills to Master:

  • Wireframing and Prototyping Tools (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD)
  • User Research and Usability Testing
  • Interaction Design
  • Visual Design and Typography
  • Responsive Design
  • Design Systems
  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for Prototyping

Companies Hiring for UI/UX Designers:

  • Google
  • Apple
  • Adobe
  • Microsoft
  • Airbnb

Future Scope:

  • AI-Powered User Interfaces
  • Voice and Gesture-Based Design
  • Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) Design
  • Design for Wearables and IoT
  • Human-Centered AI Design
