Placement Guidance

Welcome to the Placement Guidance page. This section will provide you with the necessary resources and guidance to help you secure your dream job.

Placement Preparation Timeline

Pre-placement Preparation (1-2 years before placements)

  • Focus on building a strong academic foundation.
  • Participate in extra-curricular activities (technical fests, competitions).
  • Start exploring internship opportunities.

Pre-placement Training (6 months before placements)

  • Take part in placement preparation workshops.
  • Practice coding and problem-solving skills.
  • Focus on technical skills like programming languages, data structures, algorithms, and industry-specific tools.

Placement Season (final semester)

  • Participate in mock interviews and group discussions.
  • Research companies and their recruitment processes.
  • Customize your resume and cover letter for each company.

Types of Companies and Recruitment Process

IT Companies

Recruitment Process: Online coding tests, technical interview, HR interview. Skills to Focus: Programming, problem-solving, database management, algorithms.

Core Companies

Recruitment Process: Written test, technical interview, GD, HR interview. Skills to Focus: Subject knowledge, design thinking, problem-solving.

Startups

Recruitment Process: Aptitude tests, coding rounds, informal interviews. Skills to Focus: Versatility, adaptability, and practical skills.

Consulting and Finance Firms

Recruitment Process: Case study interviews, HR interviews, technical rounds (for tech roles). Skills to Focus: Analytical thinking, problem-solving, communication.
