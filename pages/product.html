Roadmap: Product Manager

Title: Roadmap to Becoming a Product Manager

Details: Master product lifecycle management, user research, market analysis, and cross-functional team leadership. Learn essential tools and frameworks like Agile, Scrum, and Kanban, and understand how to align product strategies with business goals.

Key Skills to Master:

  • Strategic Thinking and Business Acumen
  • Market Research and Competitor Analysis
  • Product Roadmap and Lifecycle Management
  • Agile Methodologies: Scrum, Kanban
  • Data-Driven Decision Making
  • User Experience (UX) and Design Principles
  • Communication and Stakeholder Management
